For You, Khushi

Dear Khushi,

Happy Birthday! 🎂 If you’ve made it all the way to this page, I hope you’ve smiled at least a hundred times on the way, because making you smile was the whole point of this little website.

There’s so much I want to say to you, and I don’t think one page is enough. But I’ll try.

First, I want you to know how proud I am of you. Truly, deeply proud. Proud of the person you are, proud of how you treat people, proud of how strong you’ve stayed through everything, and proud of every step you’ve taken to get where you are today.

And speaking of steps: congratulations on your first job! 💼 On 29 September you started a whole new chapter of your life. I know there were nerves, new faces and new things to learn, and you walked in anyway, with courage and that beautiful smile. That’s the Khushi I know. This is only the beginning, and your future is going to be amazing.

Thank you for being such a wonderful person. Thank you for having one of the purest hearts I’ve ever known: a heart that never thinks bad about anyone and always wishes good for everyone, even for people who don’t deserve it. Thank you for being kind when it’s easy and even kinder when it’s hard. Thank you for caring, for understanding, and for always supporting the people around you. You make this world softer just by being in it.

I love how much you love your family. The way they are your whole world says everything about the kind of heart you have. May your family always stay happy, healthy and together, and may they always be as proud of you as I am.

I love your little world too: your drawings, your creative artwork and your diary full of feelings. Never stop creating. Those pages and sketches are pieces of your heart, and they are beautiful.

And of course, I love your funny little habits: your eternal friendship with brownie cake 🍫, your famous “I won’t call first” rule 📞, and your ultra-rare “sorry” that takes a little buffering 🙈. Don’t change any of it. Those little things are exactly what make you you. And when you do realise you were wrong, you own it with such honesty. That is rare, and it is beautiful.

Never stop smiling. Your smile is one of the most precious things in this world, and it deserves to be seen every single day.

Never stop dreaming. Dream big, dream bold, dream the dreams you’re even a little scared to say out loud. You are capable of so much more than you know.

May every dream of yours come true: the big ones, the small ones and the secret ones you only write in your diary. May this new year of your life bring you success in your career, peace in your mind, good health, wonderful people and more happiness than your heart can hold.

May God bless you forever, Khushi. May He protect your beautiful heart, guide every step you take, and fill your life with love, light and endless reasons to smile.

Today is your day. Eat the brownie cake (all of it). Laugh loudly. Feel loved. Because you are, more than you will ever know. 💖

Happy Birthday Khushi ❤️

Keep shining.

Keep smiling.

Keep being YOU.

♥ Happy Birthday Happy Birthday

Khushi Kasaudhan

Thank you for being you. Here’s to your best year yet. 🥂
