The Story of Khushi

A little fairy tale about a girl whose name means happiness. Scroll slowly and let each chapter unfold.

Chapter One
🌸

Once upon a time, a girl named Happiness was born

The world didn’t know it yet, but it had just received its sweetest upgrade. Her name was Khushi, and she was going to make a lot of people smile.

Chapter Two
🤍

She grew one of the purest hearts in the world

She never thought bad about anyone. She only ever wished good for everyone. Kind, soft-hearted and understanding, she became the person people run to when they need a hug.

Chapter Three
👨‍👩‍👧

Her family became her whole universe

Above everything else, she loved her family. For Khushi, family always came first, and it always will.

Chapter Four
🎨

The artist with a secret diary

She drew beautiful things and wrote her feelings into her diary. Every sketch and every page held a little piece of her heart.

Chapter Five
🍫

She found a best friend… called Brownie Cake

Some friendships are forever. This one is also chocolatey. 😄 Brownie Cake isn’t just her favourite dessert. It’s basically her best friend.

Chapter Six
📞

The legend of the phone that never dialled first

She almost never calls first. She waits for you to call. And if she misses your call… then maybe, just maybe, she’ll call back later. 😂❤️

Somehow, it only makes everyone love her more.

Chapter Seven
🙈

The rarest word in her dictionary: “Sorry”

It takes a little buffering… ⏳ But when she realises she was wrong, she owns it with honesty. And that “sorry” is worth more than a hundred quick ones.

Chapter Eight
💪

Life tested her, and she passed with grace

Some days were hard. Some days were heavy. But Khushi stayed emotionally strong, never gave up, and kept inspiring everyone around her.

Chapter Nine
💼

29 September: her very first job

She stepped into her first job and started a whole new chapter, full of hard work, big goals and bright possibilities. Everyone who knows her is so proud. ✨

Chapter Ten
🎂

And today… the story celebrates its heroine

Today the pages turn gold, because it’s Khushi’s birthday. And the best chapters? They haven’t even been written yet.

To be continued…
